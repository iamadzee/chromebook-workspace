Offline Image Studio

Convert, resize, compress, and remove solid-color backgrounds from a batch of images right on your Chromebook. Useful for preparing uploads or editing product photos without desktop image software.

Private and free: images stay on this device. No account, upload, watermark, subscription, or internet required. Re-encoding also removes embedded EXIF metadata such as camera details and GPS coordinates.

Choose images and output settings

WebP is usually the best choice for smaller files. Quality applies to WebP and JPEG; PNG output is lossless.

Turn this on, then click the background color in the preview. Similar colors become transparent; output is PNG.

Sampled color: none

This color-based remover works best on plain or evenly lit backgrounds. It is not AI subject detection, and may remove matching colors from the subject too. The same sampled color is applied to every selected image.

Select images and turn on background removal to sample a color.

Choose images to begin. Processing is local and temporary; converted files are not saved here.

  • Supported formats depend on your browser's image decoder; common JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, and BMP images are typically supported.
  • Animated images are converted from the displayed frame only. Choose PNG to keep transparency; JPEG fills transparent areas with white.
  • Limit: 50 images, 25 MB per image, 100 MB total per batch. Very large images are resized to the chosen maximum dimension before conversion.
